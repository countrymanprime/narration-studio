title: Settings
lead: Set up recording, speech to text, downloads, delivery, the project and the credits in one place.
---

Settings has eight categories, one shown at a time, listed at the left. Press **Ctrl+,** to open it from any page. A change applies at once, with no Save button.

## Find a setting

1. Type in **Search settings** at the top right. It matches the name, the description and words you might use instead.
2. Press **Changed** to list everything that differs from its default, or **In this project** to list what this project owns or overrides.
3. Open **Advanced** at the foot of a category for the rarely changed settings.

Each row says whether it belongs to **This computer**, **This project** or **All projects**. For an **All projects** setting, press **Override for this project** to give the open project its own value.

![Settings on Recording & DAW, with the category list at the left and the search and filters above](../../../docs/images/ui/settings-recording.webp "Recording & DAW")

## Set up recording

1. Open **Recording & DAW**.
2. Under **Record with**, choose **REAPER**, **Audacity** or the **Built-in recorder**. The choice is saved with the project.
3. For REAPER, press **Link** with the project's `.rpp` file, or **Choose a project file** for another one.
4. Choose the **Microphone**. The Booth uses the same one.
5. Press **Test connection** under **Connection**.

The Booth shows the steps to load the REAPER launcher when Record is waiting for REAPER ([Booth](booth.md#connect-reaper)).

## Download models

1. Open **Models & downloads**. It lists everything the app can keep on this computer, such as the Whisper models and the preview voice.
2. Press **Download** on a row, or **Download the recommended set** when nothing is downloaded yet.
3. Choose which model is used in the category that uses it: the **Transcription model** and the Booth's **Live model** are under **Speech to text**.

Nothing downloads by itself, and removing a download never touches your projects.

## Fill in the credits

1. Open **Project** and press **Edit credits…** under **Credits**.
2. Pick a template and edit its **Name**, **Kind** (Opening, Closing or Chapter announcement) and **Body**, then press **Save template**.
3. Fill in the **Project values**, such as Title, Author and Narrator. A bracketed token such as `[Title]` in a template is filled from them.
4. Check **Preview** for any token that has no value yet.
5. Under **Retail sample**, choose the chapter and line it **starts in** and **ends in**, then press **Save sample**. It can be up to 5 minutes long.

The templates are shared by every project; the filled-in values belong to this project.

## Change the look and the keys

1. Open **Appearance & keys**.
2. Set **Theme** to **Match my system**, **Light** or **Dark**.
3. Under **Shortcuts**, press **Change** on a command, press the key or pedal, then press **Replace**.
