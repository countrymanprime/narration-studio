The Script page sits under **Prep** in the navigation. It shows the chapter list on the left, the whole book as one scrolling text in the middle, and a rail of tabs on the right.

## Move through the book

1. Press a chapter in **Chapters** to go to it. Its dot shows how far its prep is: **Not started**, **In progress** or **Prepped**.
2. Read the chapter in the reader. Each chapter opens with a card that shows its title, word count, marks and open queries.
3. Set the chapter's prep state under **My prep** on its card, or press **←** and **→**.
4. Press **Alt+↑** or **Alt+↓** to go to the previous or next chapter.
5. Press **Ctrl+F** to find words, and **B** to bookmark the line you are on.
6. Change the font, size, line spacing and column width from the **text settings** in the reader's header.

![Script page with the chapter list, the reader on Chapter 1 and the rail on its Words tab](../../../docs/images/ui/script-reader.webp "The reader, the chapter list and the rail")

## Mark up the text

1. Select words in the reader. A toolbar appears over them.
2. Press **Mark** to put one of your marks on them with a note. A query for the author is a mark too.
3. Choose **Pronunciation (a word)** under **Mark** to flag a word for its pronunciation.
4. Press **Story Bible** to make a new entry from the words.
5. Press **Look up** to open the words in the rail.

Right-click the text for the same actions. To name your marks and choose each one's colour and line, open **Mark** and choose **Define marks…**.

![The selection toolbar on one word: Mark, Story Bible, Bookmark here, Record from here and Look up](../../../docs/images/ui/script-selection-popup.webp "The selection toolbar")

## Use the rail

- **Words** lists the words marked for pronunciation in the chapter in view. A word's card has **How I will say it**, its definition and the **Forvo** and **YouGlish** pages for it.
- **Story Bible** lists the entries met in the chapter. **Open in Story Bible** goes to an entry.
- **Notes** and **Queries** list the chapter's marks with a note and its questions for the author. Press one to go to its place in the text.
- **Bookmarks** lists your bookmarks under their chapters.

## Record from the script

1. Press **Record in Booth** (the microphone) on a chapter's card to open the [Booth](booth.md) on that chapter.
2. To start at a given place, select the words and press **Record from here** on the toolbar.

> **Note:** Record the opening and closing credits as their own files, from the **Record in Booth** button on their **Credits** cards, not inside a chapter file. The credits come from the templates in [Settings](settings.md#fill-in-the-credits).
