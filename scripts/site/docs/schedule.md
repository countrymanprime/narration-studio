title: Schedule
lead: Check whether the book will be done by its due date, and keep its due date, payment and milestones.
---

Schedule sits after [Production](production.md) in the navigation. With no manuscript it asks you to import one first. Every change can be undone from the toast that follows it or from the undo history.

## Set the due date

1. Find the **Final delivery** row at the end of the milestones table.
2. Type the date's parts or open the calendar. A whole, real date is saved as soon as it is written or picked.
3. To clear the due date, empty the field and press Enter.

## Read the verdict

1. Read the pill at the top right: **On track**, **Tight** or **Behind**, with when the work would finish at the current pace. A book whose chapters are all delivered reads **Finished**.
2. Read the line under the title for the days left, or how many days overdue.
3. Open **How this is worked out** for the recording, editing and proofing left, the open pickups and **Work left**. **Your pace** and **At your pace, done** are estimates from fixed numbers, and the page says what they assume.

![Schedule with the verdict, recorded audio against the plan, how the book pays, the work left and the milestones](../../../docs/images/ui/schedule-on-pace.webp "Schedule")

## Record how the book pays

1. Choose **Paid**: per finished hour, a royalty share, or none or unspecified.
2. For a rate per finished hour, type it in the **Amount** box and press Enter or leave the box. **Value at this rate** shows the rate times the book's finished hours.

## Add milestones

1. Press **Add milestone** at the top of the table.
2. Enter the name, the due date, what it measures, a note, and whether it is done.
3. Save. The milestone's status cell reads **Late by** some days, **At risk**, **On track**, or **Not estimated** for a custom milestone.
4. Use **Edit** or **Remove** on a row to change it. Removing offers Undo.
