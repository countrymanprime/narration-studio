Production is the page a project opens on. Its **Chapter pipeline** has one row per chapter and one column per stage.

## Import the manuscript

With no manuscript imported yet, the page is the import.

1. Press **Import manuscript**.
2. On step 1 of 2, **Choose your manuscript**, drop a .docx, .md or .txt file on the zone or press **Choose a file…**. A copy is kept in your project.
3. On step 2 of 2, **Check the chapter outline**, check the proposed chapters. Press a heading's title to preview how it begins.
4. Change a row's kind if a section is **Front matter** or **Reference material** rather than a narration chapter.
5. Confirm. The import runs in the background with a live activity log. Press Close when it finishes.

![Import manuscript, step 2 of 2: the chapter outline with each heading's words and a preview](../../../docs/images/ui/production-import-confirm.webp "Check the chapter outline")

> **Note:** The import does not build the Story Bible. Open [Story Bible](story-bible.md) and use **Scan the manuscript** or **Import entries**.

## Read where the book stands

1. Read the header for the book's chapters, words and due date, and the verdict pill under it (on track, tight or behind). The pill opens [Schedule](schedule.md).
2. Read the figures: **Finished audio**, **Recording**, **Editing + proof**, **Open pickups**, **Delivery check** and **Due**. Each opens the page it counts.
3. Read **Next up** for what to do next, ranked by the hours of work it puts at risk.

![Production with the figures, the chapter pipeline and Next up](../../../docs/images/ui/production-home.webp "Production")

## Work a chapter through the pipeline

1. Find the chapter's row in **Chapter pipeline**. Each cell shows a stage done, under way or not started.
2. Press a cell to open that stage for the chapter.
3. Right-click a row for the chapter's menu, including **Recording check…** and **Set status:**.
4. When a chip on the current-stage cell says the chapter looks ready, open it and press **Confirm** to move the chapter to the next stage, or **Dismiss**. The app never changes a status on its own.

## Time your work

1. Press **Start timer** on the Next up item of the chapter being recorded.
2. Press **Stop timer** at the top of the page when you finish. Only one timer runs at a time.

## Export a status report

1. Press **Export status report**.
2. Tick the sections to include and choose HTML or JSON.
3. Press **Export**. The contracted amount and effective rate are left out unless you tick their box.
