title: Story Bible
lead: Keep every character, place and organization in the book, with how you say each name and notes on how to voice them.
---

Each entry has its names and aliases, how you say each, a description, its relationships and the chapters it appears in.

## Build the entries

1. Press **Scan the manuscript** (the wand button at the top). It reads the book and suggests new entries and facts about the ones you have.
2. Or press **Import entries** to add entries from a CSV or JSON file you made elsewhere. The dialog shows what the file would add before it adds anything.
3. Add an unusual name by hand with the **+** button. The scan favours missing a name over listing ordinary words.

Nothing builds the Story Bible on its own, not even a manuscript import.

## Review what the scan suggested

1. Press the **Needs verification** chip beside the filters to show the entries with suggestions waiting.
2. Open an entry and accept, edit or reject each item in its **Suggested** list. Press a quote to go to its line in the [Script](script.md).
3. For an entry of the **Needs Review** kind, choose the right kind from the menu beside the name.
4. Press **Mark reviewed** in the entry's header.

![Needs verification: the suggestions not in the Story Bible yet, each with its score](../../../docs/images/ui/storybible-verification.webp "Suggestions waiting for you")

## Edit an entry

1. Select an entry in the list. It opens read-only.
2. Press **Edit** (the pencil), change the entry, then press **Save** or **Cancel**.
3. To add an alias, type it in **Add a name** and press Enter. If another entry already has that name, choose **Merge into this entry**.
4. Under **Relationships**, type the relationship and press the entry's name to pick the entry it is to.

A locked entry cannot be edited until you unlock it.

![An entry in edit mode, with Save and Cancel shown](../../../docs/images/ui/storybible-entry-editing.webp "An entry in edit mode")

## Set how you say each name

1. In edit mode, type in a name's **How I say** field and press Enter. The tag then reads **Entered by me**.
2. Or press the tag to choose a source: the CMU dictionary, Wiktionary (when it is installed), eSpeak NG or Merriam-Webster online.
3. Press the play button beside the name to hear it with the local preview voice. The first time, the app asks before it downloads the voice.

## Hear voice samples

A voice sample is a stretch of your own recording that you marked in [Editing](editing.md#mark-a-voice-sample) as how a character sounded. Each character's entry lists its samples under **Voice samples**, the primary one first, marked with a star.

1. Press a sample's play button to hear it, and **Stop** to stop it.
2. Choose **Make primary** from a sample's **⋯** menu to make it the one the [Booth](booth.md#hear-a-voice-sample) plays.
