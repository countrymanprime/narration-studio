# Slices and the groomer

Read by the coordinator and by a groomer session. Workers read only their own slice issue.

## Slices

A train's work is delivered as slices, one PR by one worker each. The issue form is `.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/slice.yml`, and the comment at the top of `scripts/dev/slice-lint.mjs` defines the header's keys.

- **One issue per slice (D-1).** It is a sub-issue of its PRD's issue, titled `SL-<code><n> <verb phrase>` (the prefix `scripts/dev/slice-lint.mjs` checks). The PR body says `Closes #<slice>`, and one more `Closes` line for any other issue it fully resolves.
- **The header.** A fenced `slice-meta` block with `id`, `lane`, `kind`, `depends`, `owns` and `serial`, checked by `scripts/dev/slice-lint.mjs`. The issue form is `.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/slice.yml`. The coordinator reads the header and the labels, never the code.
- **Labels:** `slice`, `lane:<L>`, `prd:<code>`, and one of `ready`, `blocked` or `owner-decision`.
- **Ready.** A slice is ready when every issue in its `depends` is closed and no other slice in progress has an overlapping `owns`. A slice waiting on an unanswered `owner-decision` is not ready.
- **Slice kinds.** The groomer cuts no `adr` slices. An open product question is answered in the PRD's decisions log or put to the owner on the decisions register; the whole-repo decisions are in `docs/adr/` and a slice does not add one.
- **A spike slice** writes a research note in `docs/research/` only when its findings outlast the slice. The note carries a date, the question, the method and the findings, and no slice, stream or lane header; the slice issue links the note, not the other way round. Findings that only the slice needs go in the slice issue.
- **An audit or checklist for one PR** is a comment on that PR or its issue, not a file.
- **A swap slice** replaces its page and deletes the old page and what only it used, in the same PR with no flag. Its `owns` lists the files it deletes.
- **Handoff.** Every slice issue has a `## Handoff` comment that the worker rewrites at each checkpoint: done, next step, failing check, files touched, decisions assumed. A resume session starts from it and the branch.
- **The groomer (D-2).** A session type for cutting slices, like the mockup-drafting session. It opens issues from a PRD's slice table (a PRD without one, under the [PRD template](../../../../docs/prds/README.md#prd-template), is cut from its phases, and its slices exist only as issues), fills each header from the code (it may read the code; the coordinator doesn't), writes a `## Plan` comment on each slice (files, behaviours, the checks that prove them), has a fresh subagent review the plans against the PRD and fixes what it finds, runs `slice-lint`, marks `ready` the slices whose dependencies are merged, and ends. The coordinator launches one when fewer than 2 x `TARGET` ready slices exist and a PRD has slice rows, or phases, with no issue.
- **A stalled worker (D-3).** A session in `working` with no push, no issue comment and no PR event for 3 hours is stalled. `interrupt_session`, then start one resume session from the Handoff comment.
- **A session stuck on a permission prompt:** relaunch up to two more times, then the coordinator runs the slice itself with a background agent under the same session cap.
- **An idle worker** gets a new session. Idle with no PR gets its one resume session from the pushed branch. Idle with a conflicted or red PR gets a fixer straight away.
- **Merging ahead of CI.** The coordinator merges train PRs without waiting for CI and reconciles `main` in batches of four or five merges (owner direction, 2026-10-11, #1119). A reconciliation session reproduces the red checks locally, fixes them in one PR, and logs on the train issue what it fixed and what it could not run; the next `main-health` run checks those. It sits outside the merge conditions in step 4 of [the pass](coordinator.md#the-coordinators-pass).
- **Decisions (D-8).** A question for the owner is an item on the [owner board](coordinator.md#the-owner-board) and in the train issue's Owner queue, with the options, the recommendation and the default date; the owner answers on the board, or `decided: <item> A` or text on the train issue. After seven days an unanswered question takes its recommendation, and the coordinator comments the default and unblocks the slices that waited. The rule never applies to a question that would change an accepted ADR or to A1..A17 of the overview. Those stay Proposed until the owner accepts them. Workers read the Owner queue at every checkpoint.
- **Status (D-9).** The owner board's status and the train issue's log, which the coordinator rewrites when something changed: per PRD the counts of slices ready, in progress, blocked and merged, the oldest ready slice, and the critical path.
