---
title: Editing
lead: Hear a chapter against its script and edit it: what you select is what you play, delete or put an effect on.
---

Each word shows how it was heard in the last recording check, and each line has its takes under it.

## Open a chapter

1. Open **Editing** in the sidebar's Edit group, or **Open in Editing** from another page.
2. Pick the chapter with the chapter step in the header, or press **Alt+↑** and **Alt+↓**.
3. If the chapter is not linked to a track, follow the banner over the script to the audio engine panel and link it (see [Navigation](navigation.md)).
4. If the page says **Not transcribed yet**, press **Check recording**.

The legend in the header names the marks in the text: words **Not in the script**, **In the script, not read**, **Misread** and **Low confidence**.

![Chapter 1 linked to its track: the legend, the script with each line's takes chip and time, and the play bar](../../../docs/images/ui/editing-default.webp "A chapter in Editing")

## Select and play words

1. Click a word to place the cursor.
2. Shift+click another word, drag across the text or press Shift+← and Shift+→ to select a run of words.
3. Press **Play** on the toolbar under the selection. The bar at the foot plays the chapter from its recorded audio, so it works with your DAW closed.
4. Click a flagged word to open its card, and keep the word as read to clear the mark.

## Delete, mute or add an effect

1. Select the words.
2. Press **Delete** (Delete words and audio). The question names the cut and asks whether to close the gap or leave room tone. Your DAW makes the cut as one undo step; the recording file is not touched.
3. Or press **Mute** to keep the audio and silence the words.
4. Or press **Add FX** (F) and choose one of your FX chains from your DAW. It goes on the selected words only.

> **Note:** **Delete**, **Mute** and **Add FX** are off while the DAW cannot make the change, for example while REAPER is not answering. A line above the bar says why.

## Choose a take

1. Click a line's takes chip, or press **T** on the cursor's line, to open its takes.
2. Play a take to hear it. The suggested take comes first, with why it is suggested.
3. Press **Keep take** (Enter) to make it the one that plays.
4. Press **Hide others** (H) to hide the other takes from the lanes.

![A line's takes opened under it: the suggested take and each take's evidence](../../../docs/images/ui/editing-takes.webp "A line's takes")

## Mark a voice sample

1. Select the words where a character's voice came out right.
2. Press **V** (or **Voice sample** on the toolbar).
3. Check the character, and add a label and a note if you like.
4. Press **Enter** to save.

The sample is saved on your computer. Manage it in the [Story Bible](story-bible.md#hear-voice-samples) and hear it in the [Booth](booth.md#hear-a-voice-sample).
