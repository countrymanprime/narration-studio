## Use the sidebar

The sidebar on the left is grouped by production stage: **Production** (Production, Schedule), **Prep** (Script, Story Bible), **Record** (Booth), **Edit** (Editing), **Review** (Proof, Pickups) and **Finish** (Master & QC, Delivery). [Settings](settings.md) sits at the bottom, under the theme switch.

1. Press a page's item to open it. The page you are on is marked with an accent-coloured bar.
2. In a narrower window the sidebar shrinks to icons. Hover it, or tab into it, to see every label.
3. Below 860 px wide, press the hamburger button at the far left of the header to open the sidebar as a drawer.

**Proof** carries a count of the notes still open, **Pickups** the open lines of the Pickups list and **Master & QC** the chapters whose file fails a delivery check.

![The navigation sidebar at desktop width](../../../docs/images/ui/nav-sidebar-desktop.webp "The sidebar")

> **Note:** With no manuscript yet, every page but **Production** and **Settings** is shown off, with a lock. Import a manuscript on [Production](production.md#import-the-manuscript) to unlock them.

## Jump to a page or run a command

1. Press **Jump to or run…** in the header, or `Ctrl+K`.
2. Type to find a page, a chapter or a command. Type `>` to list commands only.
3. Choose the result. Escape closes the menu.

The header has no other buttons, so Back and Forward (`Alt+Left`, `Alt+Right`), Undo (`Ctrl+Z`), the undo history (`Ctrl+Shift+H`) and zoom (`Ctrl+=`, `Ctrl+-`, `Ctrl+0`) are commands in the menu, with their keys beside them. A command that cannot run now stays listed, off, with the reason under it.

![The jump menu open over the page](../../../docs/images/ui/nav-jump-menu.webp "The jump menu")

## Switch project

1. Press the project's name in the header (**Switch project**).
2. Open another recent project in the picker, or press **Back** to return to the one you have open.

## Check the audio engine

The engine chip at the right of the header names the audio engine the project records with, with a dot for how it is doing.

1. Hover the chip for the detail.
2. Press it to open the audio engine panel, where the project's DAW project file is linked or changed and chapters are linked to their tracks.
