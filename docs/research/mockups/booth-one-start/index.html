Booth: one rule for where recording starts (draft)

Draft for #3858, PR 2 of the plan posted there. The rule: a place you chose (a click, a pickup line, Record from here) wins; else where your recording ends if part of the chapter is recorded; else the beginning. The one indicator is the cursor word (the filled word with a caret), as the owner decided on 2026-11-16: no chip, no extra footer or top-bar item. Before a take the cursor is where Record starts, so there is nothing else to read. The sample text is invented.
