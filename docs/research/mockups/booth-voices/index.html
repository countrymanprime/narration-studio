Booth: the voice control

One control beside Cold read shows the armed voice: its number-pad key, the character's SpeakerTag (the Narrator child shows "Narrator") and the REAPER arm state read from chapter_track_state. It never follows the speaker on the page (decision 6). Pressing it opens the voice list. While recording, a voice key switches the armed child without stopping (phase 0, row 7). The key strip gains three hints. The rail's "In this chapter" rows gain the character's voice key, or an add-track icon button where none exists.

Key order

Keys follow the child order in REAPER: Narrator is 0 wherever it sits, the other children take 1–9 in order. A tenth voice and later have no key and are reached with Num +, Num − or the list.

Who is in it?

Two dialogs, both only while stopped. Who is in it? opens on a number key with no child behind it (Num 5 here, with four voices): it lists the chapter's characters, those with lines in this chapter first, and makes one track for the ones ticked. Two or more ticked make one shared track, named "Extras" unless renamed. The new track becomes the next child, so it takes the next free key, Num 5. Who records the King? opens when the narrator chooses a character with no track (from the list or the rail): make a track (default), assign the character to an existing track, or record on the Narrator.

No track for a character

During a take nothing asks: choosing the King, who has no track, arms the Narrator child at once and the voice control shows "King → Narrator" in the warning style. That control has a fixed width, so the notice never moves the bar (frontend-primitives rule 7). After the stop the control offers the two other answers as icon buttons. What was recorded stays on the Narrator child. A key with no track while recording is refused with a toast and the take goes on. A chapter whose characters have no track shows it only in the rail's rows (the add button in place of a key, in the first section), with no extra notice.

Extras tracks

A child that holds several characters ("Extras", "TV", "Gardeners") shows a group mark and its characters. Choosing any of them arms that child. The match is the narrator's assignment, kept by the child's name, so every chapter folder with a child named "Gardeners" uses it.

Engine: linking

Three changes to the Engine panel. Chapter sync gains the project's layout, Flat or Voice folders (decision 7; a project that has one is never converted). With Voice folders, Chapter links offers folders (today they are disabled as "No audio of its own"). A new Voices card shows the children of the linked folder of the chosen chapter: key, track, the characters matched by name or assigned, and an edit icon to change them. The Narrator row can also name a character, for a first-person book (decision 2).

Decisions for the owner

  1. The voice control on the record bar, beside Cold read, as drawn (recommended), or a strip of one icon button per voice.
  2. Opening a chapter whose characters have no track: a press on each rail row's add button (drawn), or make the tracks with no press.
  3. A number key with no track while recording: refuse with a toast (drawn), or open the voice list.
  4. The Voices card on the Engine panel (drawn), or the same table inside the Chapter links row.
  5. The live switch leaves about 64 ms unrecorded at a 1024-frame buffer (phase 0, row 7): accept it, since the key is pressed between lines (recommended), or arm first and trim the overlap after the stop (row 8).
