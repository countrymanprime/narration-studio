# Workflow: Recording, Pickups, and Comping

## Goal

Turn retakes and repeated reads into organized comparison candidates without making automatic editorial decisions.

## Flow

1. Record narration and pickups in the [Booth](../guides/using-the-app/booth.md): a retry becomes a take of the item, or a
   retake on a fixed lane.
2. Open a line's [takes on Editing](../guides/using-the-app/editing.md#takes): the item's other takes and the line's other
   retakes, each with what share of the script it read as written and its pause, level, noise, clarity and breaths.
   Nothing is ranked.
3. Hear them, then **Keep take** to make one the one that plays.

A read on a separate pickup track is not grouped with the chapter's reads: the take review that did that was
[removed](../utilities/take-review.md) on 2026-11-03.

## Safeguards

- Do not treat repeated prose, echoed dialogue, or deliberate rehearsal as duplicates without a confidence threshold and context.
- Never insert, move, delete, or activate a take automatically.
- Do not compare takes with mismatched manuscript spans as though they were alternatives.

## Success signals

- A likely pickup is found faster than manually hunting through a session.
- A narrator can audition alternatives in context and explain why one was selected, from evidence that is never collapsed
  into one score.
