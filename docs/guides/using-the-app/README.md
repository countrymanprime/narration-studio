# Using the app

A screenshot walkthrough of the narration workspace, one page per app page. The screenshots
are generated from the same mock data used by the app's Playwright visual test suite
(`apps/ui/tests/visual/`) and are refreshed from its captures when the UI changes.

## Pages

Read them in order the first time, or jump straight to the page you need.

| Page | What it covers |
| --- | --- |
| [Getting started](getting-started.md) | The project picker shown before a project is open. |
| [Navigation](navigation.md) | The sidebar, its icon-only and drawer layouts, and locked entries; the header's engine chip and the audio engine panel it opens: the linked REAPER project, its tracks and chapter links, chapter sync, and the REAPER tools (chapter stamps, chapter render setup, chapter tags, cleanup tools, retakes on lanes). |
| [Production](production.md) | The page a project opens on: importing the manuscript, hours logged by stage, PFH and effective rate from measured audio, the delivery date, which chapters to work on next, and the chapter pipeline whose cells open each chapter's track, recording check, stage suggestion and editing check. |
| [Schedule](production.md#schedule) | The book's dates, payment and milestones, documented with Production. |
| [Script](script.md) | Prep: the chapter list with each chapter's prep state, the continuous reader with your marks, speakers and bookmarks, and the rail of words, Story Bible entries, notes, queries and bookmarks. |
| [Story Bible](story-bible.md) | Characters, places, and organizations with how you say each name, notes, and the suggestions a scan or an import offers. |
| [Booth](booth.md) | Where you record: a live read-along that follows you as you narrate a chapter, with companion mode beside your DAW. |
| [Editing](editing.md) | A chapter's script as the editor: what was heard set against each word, the takes of each line, deleting words with their audio, effects on words, the clean-up drawer and the timeline. |
| [Proof](proof.md) | Every check's notes in one list, all chapters or one, with the chapter's recording check and waveform, the evidence and your pickup, edit, waive or defer decision on each; and the chapter view, for comparing the recording to the manuscript. |
| [Pickups](pickups.md) | Every line to record again, from the recording check, your Proof notes and lines you add, grouped by chapter, with the pickup session that opens them in the Booth. |
| [Master & QC](master-and-qc.md) | Finish: checking rendered files against a platform's delivery profile, why a file fails, the book's consistency, the mastering chain, mastering and encoding to spec, building packages for several platforms, diagnostics, and exporting a report. |
| [Delivery](master-and-qc.md#delivery) | Finish: choosing and editing the project's delivery profile, its rules and their sources, which chapters pass, and building the platform's package. Documented with Master & QC. |
| [Settings](settings.md) | Global and per-project settings, appearance, and clearing project data. |
